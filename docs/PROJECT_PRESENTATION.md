8. **Reports and analytics** - Excel/PDF/CSV, risk, trend, projection, anomalies (screenshots).
9. **Security and testing** - Identity, CSRF, audit log, unit tests.
10. **Limitations and future work** - REST API/mobile, student portal, notifications, real ML.
